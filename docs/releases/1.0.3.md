# 1.0.3 — queued

**State:** queued behind 1.0.2.

## What changed

### Added

- Added swarm chat in the main chat: with swarm on, /swarmchat shows board
  messages as they arrive, with coloured names, landing between the lead's
  blocks and never mid-sentence. /swarmchat alone toggles it for the
  session; /swarmchat all, team <name> or main chooses which new messages
  show.
- Added captains, named by the lead for a team of five or more crewmates, or
  for a smaller team when you ask or a crewmate asks and the lead agrees; a
  captain gathers its team's work and reports it to the lead.
