# 1.0.3 — queued

**State:** queued behind 1.0.2.

## What changed

### Added

- Added swarm chat in the main chat: with swarm on, board messages show as
  they arrive, with coloured names, landing between the lead's blocks;
  messages that arrive during the lead's turn move into an expandable count
  after it. /swarmchat alone turns it off for the session; /swarmchat all,
  team <name> or main chooses which new messages show.
- Added /general, /team and /whisper to post directly to crewmates: every
  crewmate, one team (its captain, when it has one), or one crewmate, with
  names completing as you type. Your posts carry your own label and reach
  the lead too; ordinary prompts still go only to the lead.
- Added captains, named by the lead for a team of five or more crewmates, or
  for a smaller team when you ask or a crewmate asks and the lead agrees; a
  captain gathers its team's work and reports it to the lead.
- Added declared waits: a parked crewmate waiting for another crewmate's
  post, a file or subject, a message kind or its team's traffic is resumed by
  a matching message or a post addressed to it, with the message that woke
  it; broadcasts do not resume anyone.
- Added an OCR row to mercury health: the engine in use (Vision, Windows OCR
  or Tesseract) with its languages, or why none is available.

### Changed

- Changed Swarm to be on for new sessions by default; /swarm off turns it off
  for a session and /swarm on turns it back on. Resumed sessions keep their
  choice. The Swarm row in /config is the master switch.
- Changed Crew chat in the Workbench to wrap each message to three lines in
  the timeline, with the full text in the thread.
- Changed /crew to say why a parked crewmate is waiting: the post it waits
  for, or a worktree that is missing.
- Changed housekeeping to keep Node's compile cache under 512 MiB and 8,192
  files, retiring the oldest entries at start-up.
- Changed the model retirement notice to know Anthropic's current dates:
  Claude Sonnet 4.5 retires on November 30, 2026, and Claude Opus 4.1,
  Sonnet 4 and Opus 4 are retired.

### Fixed

- Fixed a crew-board notice or a finished command's output landing in the
  composer after a sign-in failure.
- Fixed a resumed session forgetting its swarm switch; /swarm on survives a
  resume.
- Fixed command messages after the last reply disappearing when a session
  is resumed or opened in another window.
- Fixed a slash command typed while Mercury is working being sent to the model
  as text; it now runs after the turn, in the order typed.
- Fixed a stray line beginning xt does not name an installed version after a
  refused update rollback on Windows.
- Fixed a check that started before an edit and finished after it counting as
  verification of the edited tree; the evidence now binds to the tree the
  check ran on.
