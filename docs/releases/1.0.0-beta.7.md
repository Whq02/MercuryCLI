# 1.0.0-beta.7 — in progress

**State:** the next tag of the public line. Its changes land on the day-to-day
line and are listed under 1.0.0-beta.7 in the release notes the product shows;
this page names the headline items and what stays queued. The verdict, the
tag commit and the publish line are written at the tag.

**Planned for this tag — a small update.** Four items:

1. **Samples** — a page the model draws (a design at true cell size, a report,
   a comparison, a small interactive page) and presents under the composer as
   a row with its own mark and the name the model gave it. Enter opens it in
   your browser. On the page you click a spot and write a comment, add a note,
   approve or ask for changes, and your marks come back into the session; the
   open tab refreshes as the model redraws; every version is kept per session,
   with a version switcher on the page and `/samples` to list them. The
   model draws one only when you ask to see something, never on its own.
   Local only: the page is a file on your machine and nothing is hosted.
2. **The "may be stuck" notice waits five minutes.** The status row said a
   quiet stream might be stuck after less than a minute of silence on most
   routes, while long silences are normal when a model thinks. Every
   90-second stream watchdog becomes five minutes (ten in patient mode) and
   the notice never comes before five minutes of silence; the OpenAI route
   keeps its fifteen-minute watchdog.
3. **Windows feels slow — measured, then fixed.** An audit on a real Windows
   box records where the time goes (typing, streaming, tool rows, screen
   switches, scrolling, boot; the processes at rest and mid-turn; the
   terminal's own share); the fixes are sized to what it shows, and none
   trades something else away to get its number.
4. **Computer use is on by default.** The Computer tool is in the model's
   toolbox from the first boot on a machine that has the desktop driver; the
   first act in each application still asks by the application's name, and
   the Boot Menu carries a row to turn it off for new sessions.
   `MERCURY_COMPUTER_USE=0` still removes it.
