# 1.0.8 — ready for the tag

**State:** ready for the tag. Computer use acts on what the page hides: a
target scrolled out of view is scrolled into view and acted on in one act,
after the session's claim and the challenge check, on macOS and on Windows,
where a row hidden by its pane and a window taller than the screen are now
seen; the sequence state line tells the truth about the whole sequence; the
side jobs' effort floor follows the words a provider serves; the word sample
reaches the Samples feature; /usage shows the TensorFold engine a session runs
on; and four text scanners that walked a long digit run quadratically are
linear.

## What changed

- Added a one-line reminder to the model when the first message you type in a
  session says "sample" or "samples": what a sample is, how it is made in an
  Eval cell, and whether Samples is on in the session (again after a
  compaction); the Eval tool's roster words name sample() when Samples is on,
  and the Boot Menu row's off words carry the one exception to "identical to a
  build without samples".
- Added a TensorFold slot to /usage's Local models block when the session's
  model is one TensorFold serves: the loaded model, its address, served window
  and memory in use, serving, draining, loading or not served, an idle mark
  until the first turn, and the ledger lines under it; a rented TensorFold pod
  keeps its ledger lines; a session on another local server shows no slot.
- Changed the side jobs' effort floor: low wherever the provider serves it,
  otherwise the served word nearest to low, the cheaper of two equally near,
  so a Mistral or Cohere list that serves only none and high runs titles,
  summaries and judges at none; the Cohere wire now sends that word (it sent
  high whenever thinking was on).
- Changed the Computer tool's edge rule: a target a browser clamps to a strip
  at a pane's edge (an element scrolled out of view) is scrolled into view and
  acted on in one act, after the session's claim and the challenge check, and
  the result says scrolled into view first; an element still hidden after the
  scroll, or a pane that will not scroll, is refused with the next move named;
  a human-verification checkbox scrolled below the page is a challenge like a
  visible one; identical siblings resolve apart by their order in the window
  and keep separate refs; a find that matches nothing keeps your refs; a ref
  asks for a fresh find once its list has changed.
- Changed wait with until to wait 5 s by default (a plain pause keeps 1 s) and
  to say how long the condition took.
- Changed the Computer tool's words: fill and fields are taught; a role shown
  with its subrole answers to either word; an element without a title is
  matched by its value; focusApp takes capture, settleMs and label and returns
  a screenshot after the switch.
- Changed four text scanners that walked a long digit run quadratically (the
  terminal's mouse-fragment and orphan-probe regexes, the capacity wait
  sentence, the TensorFold refusal reader) to linear forms with the same
  answers on every input, removed three casts that erased a type the module
  already had, and merged two repeated bodies into the helper they repeated;
  nothing a user or a model sees changes.
- Fixed the crew view's slash commands: with a crewmate opened in its view (or
  pinned as the main chat), /model, /effort, /compact, /context and /usage and
  the model-picker key act on that crewmate (its model and effort change at
  its next turn, or at its next resume once it has finished, and the status
  row shows the change waiting beside the running model; its transcript
  compacts the same way; its own numbers show), the main chat's model, effort,
  thinking and transcript stay as they were, and every other command runs for
  the whole session and says so (typing /model at a selected crewmate changed
  the main chat's model).
- Fixed window screenshots on Retina displays: the capture of a window was
  taken from twice the window's offset and twice its size (a window at y 122
  was pictured from y 244, so a click mapped from its screenshot landed 122 pt
  above the target); full-display screenshots were right, and a window at the
  top of the screen hid the fault.
- Fixed two misses of that rule on Windows: a row scrolled out of a page's
  inner pane, which Windows lists with its full layout rectangle and no pane
  between it and the page, now counts as out of view by the window's own
  off-screen report (find and tree mark it scrolled out of view), is scrolled
  into view and clicked where it lands, and a row cut by its pane's edge is
  clicked at the point Windows reports inside the part on view; and a target
  whose frame lies inside the window but past the screen's working area (a
  window taller than the screen, on any platform; the taskbar's strip
  included, unless the window covers its whole display) is refused with the
  display's edge named instead of a click the taskbar takes; a window spanning
  two displays is on view on both where windows span displays; a refusal after
  a scroll says whether the target moved; a scroll pointed past the screen
  wheels at the window's nearest point on it.
- Fixed the Windows tree's values and the switch role: text fields,
  checkboxes, sliders and selections read their value (every control read an
  empty one), and a switch reads as AXCheckBox (AXSwitch) as it does on macOS.
- Fixed the sequence state line: it compares the screen before the first step
  with the screen after the last and carries any step's change (a sequence
  ending in a screenshot said "not compared · focus not read"; one ending in a
  wait said "unchanged" after earlier steps had changed the window); a step
  that scrolled its target into view no longer hides a focus the click then
  moved).
- Fixed a screenshot step inside a sequence lending its coordinates to the
  next step: the step after it ran against the previous screenshot's map while
  the screenshot's line said the coordinates now referred to the new window; a
  pixel step after an in-sequence screenshot is now refused with the reason (an
  image the model has not seen; take the screenshot alone, then act), and the
  screenshot's own line stays in the answer.
