# 1.0.0-beta.19 — queued

**State:** queued behind 1.0.0-beta.18. This release makes the Anthropic
model list live: every signed-in account's models are read from the
provider, a new model is served the day it appears under its own name, and
sign-in and account handling across providers is brought to a frictionless
state. With it, a design pass: the large critters retire, every theme gets
its own muted accent, panels close on a click outside them, and a burst of
tool calls paints as one card that counts up. The items queued for it are
listed on the 1.0.0-beta.18 page under "Queued for 1.0.0-beta.19".

1.0.0-beta.20 is an improvement push on breadth and hardening.
1.0.0-beta.21 brings benchmarks, or the fast decision model as a flagged
beta. 1.0.0-beta.30 is the release candidate for leaving beta. Grok and
Muse will be added where they fit; Perplexity follows after beta.
